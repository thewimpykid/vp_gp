================================================================================
  NQ 0DTE GEX PROFILE — HOW IT WORKS
  gex_profile / gex_profile.py
  Last updated: 2026-06-10
================================================================================

This system identifies high-probability reversal zones on NQ futures (Nasdaq-100
E-mini) using stacked 0-DTE Gamma Exposure profiles from the 5 prior trading
sessions, combined with the same structural levels used by stacked_vp. Zero
look-ahead — all inputs are strictly prior data. Zones computed before market
open reflect only what a live trader would have known that morning.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. WHAT IS GEX AND WHY DOES IT MATTER FOR NQ?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Gamma Exposure (GEX) measures how much delta-hedging activity a market maker
must do as the underlying moves. When NQ moves through a price level where many
0DTE options are struck, dealers must rapidly buy or sell futures to stay
delta-neutral. This forced hedging creates predictable order flow:

  Positive GEX zone (large gamma): dealers buy when price falls, sell when
    price rises. Price gets "pinned" — the zone acts as a magnet and absorber.

  Negative GEX zone (low gamma): dealers amplify moves — buy when price rises,
    sell when price falls. Price accelerates through these zones.

0DTE options (expiring same day) have the highest gamma of all contracts:
gamma spikes dramatically near ATM and collapses to near-zero more than ~2σ
away. This means 0DTE GEX is tightly concentrated around the prior session's
close, not spread across a wide range. The concentration is what makes 0DTE GEX
useful — it pins highly specific price levels rather than creating vague ranges.

NQ specifically: NQ has much higher implied volatility than SPX per index point
(σ_NQ ≈ VIX × 1.15), so the 0DTE gamma bell is wider in absolute points but
still tightly centered. At VIX=18, σ_daily ≈ 287 points. Levels within ±1σ
(~287 pts) of the prior close carry most of the 0DTE gamma weight.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  2. DATA AND INPUTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

NQ 1-minute OHLCV: 1Min_NQ.csv — Sierra Chart export, 2014–2026, 4.17M bars.
  Format: semicolon-separated, European notation (3.575,75 = 3575.75).
  Cache: parsed to Parquet on first load for fast subsequent reads (<1s).

VIX daily: fetched from Yahoo Finance (^VIX), cached to vix_daily.parquet.
  Used to set σ_NQ for each session's gamma kernel.
  NQ_IV_MULT = 1.15: NQ implied vol ≈ VIX × 1.15 (empirical constant).
  If VIX is unavailable for a date, default σ = 20.0 (VIX=20 assumed).

Working range: 2020–2026. Pre-2020 excluded to match current volatility regime.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  3. THE 0DTE GAMMA KERNEL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

For each prior session, the system computes a GEX-weighted volume profile.
The core transform is:

  GEX(K) = VP_volume(K) × Γ_BS(S, K, T=1/252, σ_NQ)

Where:
  VP_volume(K) = volume traded at strike/price K during that session
                 (OHLCV approximation: volume uniformly distributed across
                  all 5-point bins spanning each bar's [Low, High])
  Γ_BS         = Black-Scholes gamma at T = 1 trading day (1/252 years)
  S            = that session's closing price (spot price)
  K            = each 5-point price bin
  σ_NQ         = that session's implied volatility (VIX × 1.15 / 100)

Black-Scholes gamma formula:
  d1 = [ln(S/K) + (r + 0.5σ²)T] / (σ√T)
  Γ  = N'(d1) / (S × σ × √T)
  where N'(d1) = standard normal PDF at d1

T = 1/252 means this models options with exactly 1 trading day to expiry.
At T=1/252 gamma is extremely peaked near ATM and decays fast:

  At NQ=22000, VIX=18 (σ_NQ=0.207), σ_daily = 22000×0.207/√252 ≈ 287 pts:
    K = 22000 (ATM)        → gamma near maximum
    K = 21713 or 22287     → gamma ≈ 60% of ATM (1σ away)
    K = 21426 or 22574     → gamma ≈ 13% of ATM (2σ away)
    K = 21139 or 22861     → gamma ≈ 0.3% of ATM (3σ away)

Effect: the GEX profile has a tight bell centered on the session close. Only
levels within ~2σ_daily of the prior close carry meaningful GEX weight. This
automatically focuses the analysis on the relevant price range for the next day.

Rescaling: GEX_raw is renormalized so its peak matches the raw VP peak volume.
This keeps GEX values on the same scale regardless of σ or S, allowing
consistent HVN/LVN detection thresholds across different vol regimes.

Fallback: if the gamma kernel produces a degenerate result (near-zero sum),
the raw VP is used directly. This handles edge cases like VIX spikes.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  4. PER-SESSION FEATURE DETECTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Each session's GEX profile (bins, gex_vols) is analyzed with Savitzky-Golay
smoothing (polyorder=2, window=max(5, n_bins//15)) before peak detection.
Smoothing prevents noise peaks in the GEX curve from generating spurious levels.

4a. VAH / VAL — GEX Value Area High / Low
    Weight: 1.2 each
    The price range containing 70% of total GEX weight (VALUE_AREA_PCT = 0.70).
    Built greedily: repeatedly add the highest-GEX bin until 70% is accumulated.
    VAH = top of the highest-included bin. VAL = bottom of the lowest.
    Interpretation: where 70% of the day's 0DTE hedging pressure was concentrated.
    Price outside the GEX value area tends to get rejected back toward it —
    the same mean-reversion logic as volume profile VA, but gamma-weighted.

4b. POC — GEX Point of Control
    Weight: 1.1
    The 5-point bin with the highest GEX weight. The "gamma-weighted fair value"
    for that session — where dealer hedging was most intensive. Price gravitates
    toward the POC during consolidating sessions.

4c. HVN boundaries — Shelf / Ledge
    Detection: scipy.signal.find_peaks on smoothed GEX, prominence ≥ 30% of peak.
    For each peak, half-width boundaries are extracted (at rel_height=0.5).
    Each boundary is classified by its local slope over 5 bins:

    Shelf (weight 0.8): avg |Δgex| / peak ≤ 0.08 — gradual taper.
      The GEX bell-curve shoulder. Price decelerates here; less sharp than ledge.
      INCLUDE_SHELF = True (unlike VP where shelf was disabled — GEX bell curves
      produce more meaningful shoulders than volume profile shelves do).

    Ledge (weight 1.3): avg |Δgex| / peak ≥ 0.20 — abrupt cliff.
      The sharpest boundary of a GEX peak. Strongest 0DTE barrier —
      dealers face the most abrupt hedging requirement change at ledge transitions.
      INCLUDE_LEDGE = True.

4d. LVN — Low Volume GEX Node
    Weight: 0.9
    Valleys in the smoothed GEX where value < 40% of mean GEX (LVN_DEPTH = 0.40).
    Low-GEX zones = thin dealer interest = fast travel or reversal magnet when
    approached from the other side. Same logic as VP LVN, applied to GEX.

4e. GWall — Gamma Wall (GEX-specific, not in VP)
    Weight: 1.1
    Peaks in |dGEX/dK| — where the GEX gradient is steepest.
    Detection: find_peaks on abs(gradient), prominence ≥ 25% of max abs-gradient,
    minimum distance = max(3, n_bins//20), smoothed GEX at peak ≥ 15% of peak.
    The top 3 prominent gradient peaks are taken per session.

    Why it matters: where GEX changes fastest with price, dealer delta-hedging
    activity changes most abruptly. A GWall is a price boundary where market
    maker behavior flips fastest — the strongest mechanical resistance/support
    in the 0DTE framework. Compared to a ledge (shape-based), a gwall is
    gradient-based — captures the same steep-slope concept differently.

    Confirmed to appear in 90%+ of sessions in recent NQ data.

4f. GFlip — Gamma Flip / Inflection Point (GEX-specific, not in VP)
    Weight: 1.3
    Inflection points of the GEX curve: where d²GEX/dK² changes sign.
    Detection: sign changes in second derivative of smooth GEX, filtered to
    candidates where local GEX ≥ 20% of peak AND local gradient is non-trivial.
    Top 2 strongest candidates taken per session (ranked by gex × |grad|).

    Why it matters: at an inflection point, the dealer's gamma curve transitions
    from accelerating to decelerating (or vice versa). This is where 0DTE dealer
    behavior switches mode — from aggressive hedging to passive. In practice,
    gflip levels often align with GEX peaks or with the transition between the
    positive-gamma and near-zero-gamma zones.

    Weight 1.3 matches ledge and pdh — high conviction because it identifies
    a regime change in dealer mechanics, not just a level of activity.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  5. FIVE-SESSION STACKING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The last N_SESSIONS = 5 prior completed sessions are processed independently.
Each session gets a unique timeframe ID: "0dte_d-1" (most recent) through
"0dte_d-5" (oldest). "d-1" = yesterday's session.

Why 5 sessions?
  One session = current GEX snapshot. Possibly random, possibly meaningful.
  Three sessions agreeing = structural. The same level was important to 0DTE
  dealers on three separate days — that's not noise.
  Five sessions = ~1 full calendar week. Captures the standard options week
  (Mon–Fri 0DTE cycle). Levels that persist across the full week have held up
  through different vol conditions and underlying price moves.

  Scoring by n_tf²:
    1 session: score × 1² = 1.0× boost
    2 sessions: score × 2² = 4.0× boost
    3 sessions: score × 3² = 9.0× boost
    4 sessions: score × 4² = 16.0× boost
    5 sessions: score × 5² = 25.0× boost

  A level that appears in all 5 prior 0DTE profiles is 25× more confident
  than one that appeared only yesterday. These "sticky" GEX levels represent
  price ranges where dealer books have been consistently active — the deepest
  liquidity pockets in the nearby range.

Only completed sessions are used. The current (possibly partial) session is
excluded. This is critical for zero look-ahead compliance.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  6. STRUCTURAL LEVELS (SHARED WITH STACKED_VP)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The same structural levels as stacked_vp are added to the GEX feature pool.
These are calendar/session-derived price boundaries, independent of VP or GEX:

  PDH / PDL  — Prior Day High/Low (last 10 days)       weight 1.4
  IBH / IBL  — Initial Balance High/Low (last 3 days)  weight 1.6
  ONH / ONL  — Overnight High/Low (last 3 days)        weight 1.2
  Gap        — Prior RTH close (last 3 days)            weight 1.1
  PWH / PWL  — Prior Week High/Low                     weight 1.2
  PMH / PML  — Prior Month High/Low                    weight 1.3
  Round      — 500pt psychological levels               weight 1.5
  Round      — 100pt psychological levels               weight 1.0

Per-day timeframe IDs (d-1, d-2, d-3…) apply to PDH/PDL, IBH/IBL, ONH/ONL,
and gap, just as in stacked_vp. This means three days making their high near
the same price gives three separate timeframes, boosting n_tf and score.

IBH/IBL carry the highest weight (1.6) because they backtested with the
strongest solo reversal rate in NQ. The Initial Balance first hour (09:30–10:30
ET) is the most-watched intraday reference in Market Profile theory.

PMH/PML require a fully completed prior calendar month — no partial months.
RTH close (gap level) uses the last bar in the 09:30–16:15 ET window only —
not the overnight close — to avoid leaking pre-market data into the RTH gap.

Round numbers are generated within ±4 × ATR20 of current price. ATR20 is
computed from the last 20 daily bars (true range, not just H-L).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  7. CONFLUENCE STACKING (THE CORE LOGIC)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

All features from all 5 GEX sessions + all structural levels are pooled.
The stacking algorithm identifies price clusters where multiple independent
sources agree — the "weight of evidence" principle.

Stacking algorithm (greedy clustering):

  1. Sort all features by price ascending.
  2. Scan left to right. For each unassigned feature, grab all others within
     ±tolerance. Tolerance = STACK_TOLERANCE × BIN_SIZE = 3.0 × 5.0 = 15 pts.
  3. All grabbed features form one cluster. Cluster price = mean of member prices.
  4. Count unique timeframes (n_tf). Features from the same timeframe count as 1.
  5. Score = n_tf² × Σ(feature weights)

The n_tf² exponent rewards multi-source alignment exponentially:

  Single source (1 TF), weight sum 4.0  → score = 1 × 4.0 =  4.0
  Two sources   (2 TF), weight sum 4.0  → score = 4 × 4.0 = 16.0
  Three sources (3 TF), weight sum 4.0  → score = 9 × 4.0 = 36.0
  Five sources  (5 TF), weight sum 4.0  → score = 25 × 4.0 = 100.0

Typical zone scores range from ~8 (single-TF structural) to 120+ (multi-session
GEX + structural confluence).

What makes a GEX zone different from a VP zone: a high-scoring GEX zone requires
the same price level to be mechanically important to 0DTE dealer books across
multiple sessions. This is not just "volume was here" but "dealers were forced
to hedge near this price, repeatedly." VP and GEX frequently agree on levels —
when they do, the combined confidence is highest (see combined_analyzer.py).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  8. DAILY ZONE SELECTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

From all scored zones, 5 are selected for display, using the same logic as VP:

  Step 1: Filter to n_tf ≥ 2 (drop single-source noise). Fallback to all zones
          if fewer than 4 survive.
  Step 2: Expand ATR multiplier from ±2.0 to ±5.0 (step 0.5) until ≥ 5 qualify.
  Step 3: Balance above/below current close (3 above, 2 below; fill from other
          side if one side is short).
  Step 4: Sort by price ascending.

ATR20 is used as the range window so the selection automatically scales to
NQ's current vol regime — in a 150-pt-ATR regime fewer zones qualify in a narrow
band; in a 350-pt-ATR regime the ±2 ATR window already captures plenty.

On the console output:
  "0DTE_SESS" = how many of the 5 prior sessions contributed to the zone.
  "[0dte_d-1|0dte_d-3]" = which specific sessions contributed.
  Zones showing 3+ 0DTE sessions are the highest-conviction GEX levels.

On charts:
  Zone bands centered on zone.price, band half-width = price × 0.0005 (~11 pts).
  Color: cyan gradient, darker = higher score.
  Dashed line at zone center.
  Label: price, [R/S] role, feature types, n_tf, 0DTE session count.
  Gold dotted lines: actual HOD/LOD (post-hoc reference only, not used in logic).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  10. VIX DEPENDENCY AND REGIME SENSITIVITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The gamma kernel's width is directly controlled by VIX × 1.15 / 100 = σ_NQ.

Low VIX (e.g. VIX=12, σ_NQ=0.138):
  σ_daily ≈ 22000 × 0.138 / √252 ≈ 191 pts
  The gamma bell is tighter. Only levels within ~380 pts of the prior close
  get meaningful GEX weight. Zones are more concentrated, closer to price.

High VIX (e.g. VIX=30, σ_NQ=0.345):
  σ_daily ≈ 22000 × 0.345 / √252 ≈ 478 pts
  The gamma bell widens. More of the price range gets GEX weight.
  Zones spread further from price. POC/VAH/VAL shift away from ATM.

This automatic VIX-scaling is the key advantage of the GEX approach over
raw VP: the system knows how wide dealer gamma coverage is on any given day.
During a VIX spike to 50 (e.g. Aug 2024 crash), the gamma bell becomes so
wide that GEX is nearly flat — correctly signaling "no reliable pin levels
today." Raw VP would still produce levels that don't carry gamma meaning.

AVG VIX used in reporting is the mean across the 5 prior sessions. If avg VIX
at time of analysis differs significantly from the session-specific VIX values
stored in each GEX profile, the zone's effective radius may be wider or narrower
than it was when that level was originally formed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  11. NO LOOK-AHEAD ENFORCEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Every sample-day chart and every HOD/LOD coverage test uses strict walk-forward:

  df_prior  = all bars with date < target_day
  df_window = df_prior.tail(30_000)   # ~76 trading days

  run(df_window, vix_series) → zones

The VIX lookup uses vix_series[index <= as_of_date - 1 day] — prior day's VIX
only. The day_vix for session d-1 uses the VIX as of that session's date, which
was already in the past relative to the target day.

No data from the target day or any future day enters the GEX computation.
The 30,000-bar tail comfortably covers 5 full prior sessions
(5 × 390 bars = 1,950 bars needed) plus all structural level lookbacks.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  12. HOD/LOD COVERAGE VALIDATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Method: identical to stacked_vp. For each test day (walk-forward, no sampling):
  1. Compute zones from strictly prior data.
  2. Find minimum distance from any zone to the day's actual HOD and LOD.
  3. Hit if distance ≤ 15 points (fixed, ±15pt band = 30pt zone width).

Results (2024+2025, all eligible days, ±15pt tolerance):
  2024: HOD 65.5%  LOD 73.5%  Both 48.6%  (n=313 days)
  2025: HOD 62.4%  LOD 70.1%  Both 43.1%  (n=311 days)
  Combined: HOD 63.9%  LOD 71.8%  Both 45.8%  (n=624 days)
  Avg distance to zone (combined): HOD 14.8 pts, LOD 12.8 pts

LOD capture (71.8%) is consistently higher than HOD (63.9%). Interpretation:
GEX downside levels (dealer support near prior session lows, IB lows) are more
structurally stable than upside levels, possibly because NQ selloffs are sharper
and more decisive about stopping at gamma-support, while breakouts are more
gradual and overshoot GEX resistance levels before reversing.

Miss rate (~36% HOD, ~28% LOD) is concentrated on:
  a) Trend days: price breaks far from all prior structure. Correct behavior.
  b) Gap-and-go opens that exceed the prior week's range entirely.
  c) VIX spike days where the gamma bell was too wide to pin any level.
  d) Low-volume sessions (holidays, half-days) with compressed ranges.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  13. COMPARISON WITH VP (STACKED_VP)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

GEX outperforms VP on HOD/LOD coverage by ~+5% HOD, +6-7% LOD (2024+2025).

Why GEX often beats VP:
  - VP captures where volume was. GEX captures where dealer hedging was forced.
    In NQ, institutional flow creates volume at GEX levels precisely because
    dealers are printing large orders at those strikes.
  - 0DTE gamma is tightly time-bounded. VP windows (30/60/90d) can include
    irrelevant old structure. 5-session GEX stays current.
  - GWall and GFlip have no VP equivalent. They identify mechanistic dealer
    response points that VP cannot see.
  - VIX-scaling automatically filters noise when vol is high.

Where VP has advantages:
  - Longer historical structure (90d POC, quarterly value area) not in GEX.
  - Better for slow-moving structural support many weeks old.
  - Does not require VIX data.

Combined system (combined_vp_gex) gets marginal HOD/LOD improvement over GEX
alone (+0-1%), suggesting GEX already captures most of the zone structure, and
VP adds mostly redundant levels where they agree.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  14. WHAT THE SYSTEM IS NOT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Not a mechanical buy/sell signal. Zones mark elevated reversal probability
vs. a random price level — not guaranteed reversals. Require additional
triggers (pattern, delta, tape) before entry.

Not calibrated for low-VIX trending markets. If NQ's daily ATR compresses
below ~150 pts sustained, the 5-session GEX stack will still produce zones
but their predictive value degrades (fewer forced-hedge situations).

Not a substitute for reading the tape. GEX zones tell you where dealers have
been. Where they will be tomorrow depends partly on overnight news, macro events,
and options positioning data (actual OI by strike) that this system does not use.

Not designed for pre-2020 data. Pre-2018 NQ volatility (~100 pt ATR) means the
20-point gamma band at T=1/252 was proportionally much wider, making per-session
GEX bells too diffuse to distinguish levels. Use 2020+ only.
